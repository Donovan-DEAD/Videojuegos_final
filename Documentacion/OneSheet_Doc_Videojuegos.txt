Game title: Madness dreams


Target age:  15 to 21 years old


Game summary:
It is a twist on the story of Hansel and Gretel, where one of the siblings, along with other protagonists from fairy tales, becomes a victim of a superior being (to be defined) that forces them to fight each other by keeping them under its control. The player (Hansel or Gretel) will have to explore the worlds of the fairy tales and face their respective enemies and bosses. At the end of their journey, the player manages to rescue their family member and defeat the supreme being, returning to their world.


Game outline:
The player will have to advance through procedurally generated maps whose seed will change with each iteration of the game. These maps will have enemies whose mechanics and appearance will change depending on the world being visited. In order to progress between levels (the worlds), the player will have to defeat bosses, from whom they will obtain upgrades in both abilities and weapons. With each defeat, the seed of the procedural generator will change, and the progress acquired will be retained.


Unique selling points:
* Twisted fairy-tale universe
* Procedurally changing fairy-tale worlds
* Fairy-tale characters forced to fight 
* Persistent progression across runs

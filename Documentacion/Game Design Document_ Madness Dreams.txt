Game Design Document: Madness Dreams
Front End of the Game
* Engine Boot Screen: Upon launching the application, the initial splash sequence displays the Unity engine logo, establishing Unity (6.3 LTS / 2D framework) as the core development environment.
* Studio Logo Screen: Displays the THEBESTGAMEDEVELOPMENTSTUDIO branding alongside core development team credits.
* Age Rating & Content Advisory: Displays an ESRB 13+ (Teen) rating badge, citing fantasy violence and dark thematic fairy-tale elements.
* Title Sequence Transition: Following publisher, legal, and third-party software disclosures, the sequence seamlessly transitions into the Madness Dreams title presentation before loading the main interface.
Start Screen
* Visual Presentation: Features evocative key art of Hansel and Gretel standing at the threshold of deformed, dark fairy-tale worlds.
* Interface Options:
   * New Run / Start: Begins a new procedural run.
   * Character Select: Toggles the active player avatar between Hansel and Gretel.
   * Save / Load Profile: Manages save slots, seed persistence, and unlocked Tale Fragments.
   * Player Options: Audio sliders (BGM, SFX, Master), video resolution, contrast adjustments, rebindable controls, and feedback toggles.
   * Exit: Closes the application.
Game Flowchart
The structural mechanics and screen states transition as follows:
1. Startup: Engine Splash Screen    Studio Logo    ESRB 13+ Rating    Main Menu.
2. Setup: Main Menu    Character Selection (Hansel or Gretel)    Seed & Grid Initialization (RunManager).
3. Exploration Loop: Spawn in Starting Chunk    Procedural Navigation (MapGenerator)    Engage Room Enemies (ChunkContent).
4. Boss Arena Encounter: Enter Arena    Gates Seal (BossArenaController)    Defeat Realm Guardian (CaperucitaBoss / PeterPanBoss).
5. Progression Phase: Claim Persistent Upgrade (BossAbilitySO / Tale Fragments)    Gates Unseal    Regenerate Seed & Advance Realm (WorldConfigSO).
6. Climax & Finale: Complete Final Storybook Realm    Confront The Puppeteer    Sibling Rescued / Victory Credits or Game Over.
Game Camera
   * Perspective: Orthographic top-down 2D camera locked dynamically to the active player avatar.
   * Camera Logic System:
   * Uses Camera.main to calculate cursor vectors for player directional aim and ranged ability targeting.
   * Clamps camera movement within the boundary metrics of the current room chunk (MapChunkSO) to prevent rendering empty void spaces.
   * Locks camera bounds during boss encounters to keep both player and boss within the enclosed arena boundary.
HUD System
   * Health / Status Display: Heart-based counter showing active health status, expandable via upgrades.
   * Primary Attack Display: Status indicators for basic melee (Space) and ranged (K) actions, including active cooldown sweeps.
   * Ability Hotbar: Four persistent slots mapped to keys 1 through 4, presenting icons and cooldown overlays for acquired boss abilities.
   * Tale Fragment Counter: Real-time counter displaying collected memory currency dropped by defeated guardians.
   * Navigation Overlay: Minimap reflecting cleared room chunks, current room positioning, and the location of the boss arena.
  

  



  

  

Character
   * Protagonist Selection: Hansel or Gretel.
   * Narrative Motivation & Conflict: One sibling is abducted by The Puppeteer, a manipulative entity corrupting classic fairy-tale heroes. The remaining sibling must venture through distorted realms, liberate the controlled guardians, and rescue their family member.
   * Architecture:
   * Top-down physics handled by Rigidbody2D with zero gravity scale and constrained Z-axis rotation.
   * Animation states controlled by PlayerAnimationController updating an integer parameter State (  ,   ,   ,   ,   ).
Metrics
      * Spatial & Physical Metrics:
      * Player Avatar: Fits a standard    tile area, utilizing a CircleCollider2D with clearanceRadius = 0.5 units.
      * Collision Layer: Configured under the Player layer, interacting with Ground, Walls, Enemies, EnemyAttack, Pickups, and BossTrigger.
         * Movement Metrics:
         * Walk / Chase Velocity: Base moveSpeed = 5.0 units/sec applied via FixedUpdate.
         * Combat Metrics:
         * Primary Melee: Hitbox radius meleeRange = 1.5 units, cooldown meleeCooldown = 0.5s, attack windup meleeAttackDuration = 0.3s.
         * Primary Ranged: Projectile velocity projectileSpeed = 8.0 units/sec, cooldown rangedCooldown = 0.8s, attack duration rangedAttackDuration = 0.3s.
Player Skills
         * Basic Skills:
         * Melee Strike: Bound to Space. Executes an area-of-effect check via Physics2D.OverlapCircleAll against the Enemies layer, dealing 10 base damage.
         * Ranged Shot: Bound to K. Spawns a projectile directed toward the cursor position calculated via Camera.main.ScreenToWorldPoint, dealing 5 base damage.
         * Boss-Acquired Skill Upgrades (Slots 1–4):
         * Wolf's Claw (Ability_WolfClaw): High-damage melee swipe paired with a brief forward lunge.
         * Thorn Basket / Shot (Ability_ThornShot): Ranged projectile inflicting damage and a movement debuff (slowMultiplier, slowDuration).
         * Peter's Dagger (Ability_PeterDagger): Multi-projectile fan attack firing high-speed daggers.
         * Fairy Dust (Ability_FairyDust): Mobility dash offering instantaneous short-range repositioning.
Player Equipment
         * Weaponry Structure: Dual-weapon setup integrating built-in primary melee and ranged functionality.
         * Inventory & Skill Slots:
         * Managed by PlayerAbilities, supporting up to 4 active skill slots bound to keys 1, 2, 3, and 4.
         * Defeated bosses drop collectible skill items (AbilityPickup) on the Pickups layer, automatically assigned to an open slot upon collection.
Powerups
         * State Modifiers & Persistent Drops:
         * Tale Fragments: Currency granted upon defeating realm bosses, used to purchase meta-progression traits between runs.
         * Boss Skill Drops: Unique skill items (lootPrefab) dropped by guardians, providing persistent active abilities for the duration of the run.
         * Heart Containers: Restorative items and permanent expansions for base health capacity.
Health
         * Health System Mechanics: Managed by the Health component, starting with a base pool of 6 hearts.
         * Damage Interface: Implements IDamageable.TakeDamage(), deducting HP upon contact with enemy hitboxes or EnemyAttack projectiles.
         * Low Health Warning & Death Conditions:
         * HUD provides audio-visual low-health prompts when remaining HP falls below   .
         * Depleting HP to 0 fires the Died event, triggering the Death animation state (State = 4) and concluding the active run.
            * Death Penalty: Resets the current map instance while preserving unlocked Tale Fragments and meta-progression abilities.
Game Progression Outline
            * Run Flow & Beat Chart:
            1. World 1 (Forest): Navigate seed-generated chunks    engage forest enemies    enter boss arena    defeat Corrupted Red Riding Hood    acquire Wolf's Claw or Thorn Basket    generate new seed.
            2. World 2 (Sea): Navigate ocean chunks    engage marine enemies    enter boss arena    defeat Corrupted Peter Pan    acquire Peter's Dagger or Fairy Dust    end of demo content.
            3. Full Game Expansion: Continue through remaining corrupted realms (e.g., Ice Realm, Castle Realm)    final confrontation against The Puppeteer    rescue captive sibling.
World Overview
1. Red Riding Hood's Forest (World 1 — Demo & Full Game)
               * Visual Style & Mood: Dark, atmospheric forest dense with crimson mist, twisted trunks, and ruined cottages (Palette: Red, Black, Gray).
               * Player Objective: Navigate room chunks, locate the arena, and liberate Corrupted Red Riding Hood.
               * Thematic Enemies:
               * Guard Wolf: Aggressive melee unit with high chase velocity and wide perception.
               * Living Bramble: High-health, low-speed melee unit acting as a barrier.
               * Possessed Woodcutter: Medium-range melee unit dealing heavy single-target damage.
               * Cursed Firefly: Ranged unit firing pollen projectiles.
               * Boss — Corrupted Red Riding Hood:
               * Phase 1 (   HP): Melee slash attacks combined with targeted lunges.
               * Phase 2 (   HP): Summons wolf minions and introduces ranged thorn basket attacks.
               * Phase 3 (   HP): Enrage state with increased movement speed, reduced attack cooldowns, and larger minion waves.
2. Neverland Sea (World 2 — Demo & Full Game)
                  * Visual Style & Mood: Twilight marine setting with floating islets, sunken pirate ships, and bioluminescent waters (Palette: Night Blue, Cyan, Gold).
                  * Player Objective: Traverse oceanic chunks and defeat Corrupted Peter Pan.
                  * Thematic Enemies:
                  * Cabin Boy / Lost Boy: Standard melee combatant.
                  * Mermaid: Ranged unit launching water projectiles.
                  * Tick-Tock Crocodile: High-speed, high-health melee pursuer.
                  * Dust Fairy: Fast, small-hitbox ranged unit.
                  * Boss — Corrupted Peter Pan:
                  * Phase 1 (   HP): High-speed ranged attacks utilizing thrown daggers.
                  * Phase 2 (   HP): Summons lost boy/fairy minions and fires 3-dagger fan volleys.
                  * Phase 3 (   HP): Summons a shadow minion, executes rapid dash maneuvers, and fires wide 5-dagger fan volleys.
Future Worlds & Hub Area (Full Game)
                     * Additional storybook worlds (e.g., Ice Realm, Castle Realm) accessible in sequence or through a central storybook hub, culminating in the final confrontation against The Puppeteer.
